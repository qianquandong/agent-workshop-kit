# After the session: 20 questions

When you have 20 minutes, open Claude on your phone, paste this, tap the voice button, and talk.

```
First read my Google Doc "Prep Profile – [my first name]" if I have one, and skip any question it already answers. Then interview me with the 20 questions below, one at a time. I will answer out loud. Don't comment on my answers: say "got it" and ask the next one. If an answer is very short, ask one follow-up.

My work
1. What does a great week look like for me?
2. What are my top 3 goals for the next 90 days?
3. What do I do every week that I'd love to hand off?
4. What will I never hand off?
5. When am I at my best, and when should you leave me alone?

My clients
6. Who are my best clients, and what makes them great?
7. What do clients thank me for most?
8. Why does a client leave, or a deal go quiet?
9. What do I wish I knew before every call?
10. What should you always remember about a client?

How I talk and write
11. How would a friend describe the way I talk?
12. Words I love, and words I never use?
13. Think of an email I'm proud of. What made it good?
14. How long should my emails be?

How I decide
15. What do I say yes to fast? What do I say no to?
16. When must you ask me first, and when can you just do it?
17. What mistake would upset me most?

Our team
18. Who do I go to for what?
19. What slows our team down the most?
20. If you could fix one thing for me in the next 30 days, what should it be?

When we finish, add what you learned to my Google Doc "Prep Profile – [my first name]", under a new heading "About me". If the doc doesn't exist yet, make it. Don't make a separate file. Then tell me what should change in the team-voice skill and Team Settings.
```
